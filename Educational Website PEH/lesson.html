Lesson 1

Learning Objectives

1. Clearly explain the brief history and nature of dance.
2. Appreciate the value and health benefits of dance.
3. Perform a simple dance.

Lesson 2

Learning Objectives

1. Identify the different elements of dance.
2. Show enthusiasm in learning by self assessing health-related fitness status.
3. Develop simple dance art crafts incorporating the different elements of dance.

Lesson 3

Learning Objectives

1. Identify form, motif, and phrasing in dance.
2. Explain the characteristics of a good dance.
3. Adapt the characteristics of a good dance in their daily routine in life.

Lesson 4

Learning Objectives

1. Learn about the different types of folf dance.
2. Be able to appreciate our folk dances.
3. Apply your learnings about folk dance in your life.

Lesson 5

Learning Objectives

1. Identify the two types of ballet.
2. Perform the basic arm and feet positions of ballet.
3. Be able to appreciate the intricacies of ballet.
